Transparency

No black box. Just a clear groove.

Two questions deserve a straight answer: how was this music made, and where does my subscription go? Here is how Vynl approaches both.

Labels

How a track is labeled.

Every track carries one of three labels. Creators choose it when they release, and they can add a few words to explain.

Human

Composed, performed and produced by people. No AI system had a creative hand in it.

AI-assisted

People and AI tools worked together. A person leads the work and uses AI for part of it, for instance a melody idea, a stem or a mix. The creator can describe how.

AI-generated

Produced mostly or entirely by an AI system. These tracks are welcome on Vynl. They are simply never passed off as something else.

Declared by the creator

The label comes from the person who made the track, at the moment they release it.

Kept as a history

A new declaration never overwrites the old one. Every version stays on record.

Royalties

Your listening decides who gets paid.

Vynl is built around user-centric royalties: the artists you play are the ones your subscription supports.

Pooled model

One big pot

Many services put every subscription into a single pool and split it by total streams. The music you actually play can end up funding what you never do.

User-centric · Vynl

Your money follows your ears

Your subscription is shared among the artists you played, in proportion to how much you played each of them.

Where things stand. Royalty payouts are not live during the beta. The calculation and reporting are still being built, and this page will explain exactly how it works.
