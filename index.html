Why Vynl

A quieter, more honest way to listen.

No mystery about what you're hearing, or about where your subscription goes.

Every track, labeled

Creators declare whether a track is human-made, AI-assisted or AI-generated, so you choose what you play with the full picture.

Your listening pays the artists you play

Vynl is built around user-centric royalties: your subscription follows what you actually listen to, not a global pool.

Room for every kind of creator

Independent artists, producers and AI-native creators release side by side, with the same studio and the same rules.

The labels

Three labels. No fine print.

Human

Composed, performed and produced by people. The music you'd expect from a studio, a bedroom or a rehearsal room.

AI-assisted

People and AI tools worked together. A person leads the work, and the creator can explain how AI was used.

AI-generated

Produced mostly or entirely by an AI system. Welcome on Vynl, and clearly labeled as such.
