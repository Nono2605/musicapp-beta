Human × AI

AI is part of music's future. Transparency should be part of it too.

BRAND supports every way music gets made — as long as listeners can always see how.

Human

Human-made

Music created primarily by human artists.

AI-assisted

AI-assisted

Music where AI is used as part of the creative process.

AI-generated

AI-generated

Music created primarily or entirely using AI systems.

AI creation ≠ fraud. Labeling how a track was made is about transparency, not suspicion. Fraudulent listening activity — such as bots or fake streams — is detected independently of whether the music itself was made by a human, an AI, or both.
