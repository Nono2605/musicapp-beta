Fair distribution

Fraudulent activity should not generate artificial financial rewards.

A handful of replays from a genuine fan is normal listening, not fraud — and how a track was made is a separate question entirely. AI music can be legitimate. Artificial streaming can be fraudulent. BRAND only looks at whether the activity behind a stream is real, never at who or what created the music.

Anti-concentration design

Reward loyalty without creating a winner-takes-all system.

If you spend almost all your listening time with one artist, they should benefit strongly. But as concentration on a single artist becomes more extreme, each additional share of your listening carries a little less marginal weight than the one before it.

First tranche → 100% weight Second tranche → lower weight Extreme concentration → progressively lower marginal weight

Conceptual illustration only — not a formula. Exact tranches and weights are not finalized.

The goal is not to punish successful artists. It is to reduce incentives for extreme concentration, manipulation and artificial listening while keeping genuine support valuable.
